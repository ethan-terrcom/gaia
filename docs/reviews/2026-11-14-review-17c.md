## Summary for the owner
The run page shows everything about one run:
- the ticket, status, branch, worktree (copy, or open in VS Code), PR and CI, and cost against
  budget;
- the steps with their attempts and results;
- the run's approvals;
- a **live timeline** of what the agents did, windowed so thousands of events stay smooth;
- a **diff** tab with the committed and uncommitted changes.

A running run can be cancelled behind a confirmation.

## Findings
| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Medium | The tab was local state, so the approvals inbox's "View diff" link (`/runs/<id>?tab=diff`) opened the timeline. | **Fixed:** the tab lives in the URL (`?tab=diff`). Test, and verified in the browser from the inbox link. |
| R2 | Low | Steps showed the 0-based `step_index` ("0."), while the dashboard counts from 1. | **Fixed:** steps count from 1. |
| R3 | Low | The task-start `agent.message` uses the key `message`, not `content`, so its row showed "—". Lifecycle rows repeated their type as the summary ("run.node_started run.node_started"). | **Fixed:** falls back to `message`, and node, approval and decision events are summarized from their payloads. The helpers moved to `events.ts` (fast-refresh lint rule). Test. Follow-up: one key for agent messages in the backend. |

**Checked:**
