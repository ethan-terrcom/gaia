## Summary for the owner
The approvals inbox is where you make every decision Gaia asks of you:
- read and edit plans;
- approve or reject commands, with each argument shown separately;
- approve opening a PR, seeing the lead's verdict, feedback and issues;
- approve a merge by typing `merge <n>`;
- grant more budget;
- retry or stop after an escalation.

`j`/`k` move between items, `a` approves and `r` rejects. A merge always needs the typed phrase, and
`a` never merges.

## Findings
| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Medium | After a decision the selection moved to `index + 1`. Once the refetch dropped the decided item, that **skipped the next one**. And because gate decisions apply asynchronously (202), a refetch could still list it as pending. | **Fixed:** decided items are hidden at once, and the index stays, so it lands on the next item. Test for both refetch outcomes; the test fails on the old code. |
| I2 | Medium | "Decided" fetched without a status filter, so it **included pending items**. Because the list is oldest first, it also showed the **oldest** 50 rather than the most recent. My handoff specified that. Decided items also still showed Approve/Reject. | **Fixed:** `GET /approvals` gains `decided=true` (non-pending only) and `order=newest` (backend test). Decision buttons and `a`/`r` apply only to pending items. |
| I3 | Low | `pr_open` showed only the review's feedback. | **Fixed:** it also shows the verdict and issues (`Review` is `{verdict, feedback, issues}`). Live E2E fix D put the review in the payload. |
| I4 | Info | The list shows "Run <id>" because approval payloads carry no ticket title. | Follow-up: join the ticket title into `ApprovalRead`. |
| I5 | Info | `r` rejects at once. On a plan or escalation, that ends the run. | **Accepted** per the handoff (doc 17: "approve/reject with keyboard shortcuts"). Raised with the owner as an option: confirm on `r` for plan and escalation. |
